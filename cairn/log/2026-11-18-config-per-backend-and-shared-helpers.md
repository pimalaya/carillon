# The configuration per backend, the shared helpers once

Finding CRL-9 of the family analysis (~/.claude/plans/pimalaya-cli-family-analysis-2026-11-15/plan.md section 4.5), structure only. No spec moved, and no CHANGELOG line: nothing a user or a script sees changes.

- src/config.rs held ten backends in 3215 lines with 157 `#[cfg]` attributes. Its backend items now live in src/config/, one file per block, each gated once where config.rs declares it and carrying no `#[cfg]` inside, as himalaya's src/config/ is laid out: imap.rs (the block, SASL, ID, IDLE, hooks), jmap.rs, maildir.rs, pimdir.rs, caldav.rs, carddav.rs, msgraph.rs, gmail.rs, gcal.rs, gpeople.rs, plus what several share: dav.rs (`cfg(dav)`, the server, credential and poll of both DAV blocks), domains.rs (`any(jmap, msgraph)`, a collection and hooks per domain), api.rs (`cfg(api)`, the bearer token and poll of the vendor APIs), proxy.rs and tls.rs (`cfg(network)`). config.rs keeps `Config`, `AccountConfig`, the removed and renamed keys, `Hook`, `ItemHook`, `FlagHook`, `PollWatchConfig`, `parse_url` and the tests; its 89 remaining gates are the module declarations, the built-or-unread twins of each `AccountConfig` field and the tests. The human `// NOTE` section comments moved with the items they head. Callers name the module (`config::imap::ImapConfig`), no re-export added. The move was mechanical: chunks cut at item boundaries by a script, then imports settled by the compiler across every single-feature build.
- The read loop of the coroutine pumps existed four times (`dav::pump`, `dav::pump_redirect`, `imap::pump`, `jmap::subscribe`) and `is_timeout` three times. src/read.rs now holds `SILENCE`, `is_timeout`, `read` (one read woken at each deadline, handing back bytes, a close, a shutdown or a silence) and `bytes` (the same, failing the request on anything but bytes); the three pumps call `bytes`, the JMAP event stream calls `read` with three pings as its silence. The event stream now waits in `read` across deadlines rather than resuming its coroutine on each.
- The retry of a failed round on a fresh connection existed six times (dav, jmap, msgraph, gmail, gcal, gpeople). `poll::retried` runs a round and, unless a shutdown was requested, once more on a client from `open`; the six sites call it. The JMAP `reconnect` went with it, `jmap::rounds` taking the shutdown flag, and its test became `a_round_retried_presents_the_credential_resolved_again`, a round failing 401 on the expired token and passing on the fresh one. The vendor backends now skip that retry once a shutdown is requested, as the DAV one already did. The IMAP envelope resolver keeps its own retry, which drops its client rather than replacing it.
- Kept: the two ten-way ladders, `watch::watch_session` and `account::check`. Each arm takes another config type, another method table, another collection naming and, for IMAP, a resolver; folding them needs a backend trait, a strategy trait the family's coroutine-over-trait rule turns down, for coordinator code that already reads as a table.
- Checks: fmt, clippy with every feature, with none and with each backend alone, and `cargo test --all-features`, all clean; the test count is unchanged (151 unit tests plus the integration suites).
