# The io-* releases of 2026-11-13, and watches that resume

carillon builds on io-gcal 0.4, io-gmail 0.7, io-gpeople 0.7, io-http 0.7, io-imap 0.9, io-jmap 0.8, io-maildir 0.4, io-msgraph 0.8, io-pim-discovery 0.12, io-sasl 0.2 and io-webdav 0.8, and on io-oauth 0.6 for its live tests. One io-http, one io-sasl and one io-oauth resolve. pimalaya-cli 0.3, io-pimdir 0.9 and the pimalaya-config and ceichas pins are untouched; pimalaya-stream 0.3.1 still pulls io-proxy 0.1.1, the 0.2 of the same day waiting on a pimalaya-stream release. Spec moved: `daemon` (MODIFIED: The daemon owns the connection lifecycle, A watch survives the change it reports, Microsoft Graph and Google accounts are watched through their own APIs).

- The `imap` feature enables `scram` on io-imap (family audit of 2026-11-13, SASL-S1). io-sasl carried SCRAM while io-imap was built without it, so an account configuring `imap.sasl.scram-sha-256` failed with `UnsupportedMechanism`, and nothing drew its client nonce.
- IMAP resumes across sessions. src/watch.rs keeps a `Resume` per account beside its alarm runner; src/imap.rs hands its `ImapMailboxWatchState` to `ImapMailboxWatchStreamOptions::resume_from` and takes it back from `into_state`. The state is kept when the session ended on `ImapClientError::is_connection_lost`, or on a clean end, and dropped on any refusal, `UidValidityChanged` among them, which a kept state would meet again on every reopening. A listing that skipped lines is io-imap's to handle: its watch removes nothing from one.
- The envelope resolver reads through `ImapMessageFetchFirst`, which leaves out the FETCH of any other message a server adds to its answer. The map `ImapMessageFetch` returns is keyed by sequence number, and the resolver took its first entry.
- Gmail keeps its history cursor across sessions the same way, a resumed session running its first round at once. src/gmail.rs does not use io-gmail's `GmailHistoryPoll`, so its `Expired` yield, its terminal errors and its `auth` field have no caller here: the rounds already met an expired cursor (404) with a warning and a resume from now, and a failed round already reopens the connection with a fresh token. They gain what the coroutine gained: a listing stops on a repeated page token or past `GMAIL_HISTORY_POLL_MAX_PAGES` and the watch resumes from now, and an empty history id reads as none.
- The JMAP event stream is reopened with the id of the last event read (`JmapEventSourceOptions::last_event_id`). Nothing relied on pings as a heartbeat: a frame was news only when it named a state the watch did not hold, so pings no longer surfacing changes nothing.
- No Google Calendar id was percent-encoded on carillon's side, so nothing was removed for io-gcal 0.4. io-webdav 0.8 fails a body that is not one whole multistatus, which src/dav.rs meets as a failed round, where a cut listing could read as a shorter one. io-msgraph 0.8 recipients are read through `MsgraphField::as_option`. tests/jmap.rs decodes the raw JSON `method_responses` now holds.

Verified by `cargo test --all-features` (99 unit tests, the live suites built and ignored) and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` over the default, empty and per-backend feature sets. src/imap.rs gains two tests against a scripted server (a message arriving between two connections is reported by the second; a UIDVALIDITY change drops the state), src/gmail.rs two against a scripted stream. The first IMAP test failed with the state not handed over (`left: [ItemAdded { domain: Message, id: "2" }]`, `right: []`). No watch was run against a real account, and the JMAP stream change has no test short of the live suite.

Left, each a decision of its own: moving src/gmail.rs onto `GmailHistoryPoll`, whose reason against (a private cursor) is gone, but which reads every arrival whether a hook wants it or not and groups a tick's changes by kind, losing their order; resuming JMAP, DAV, Graph, Calendar and People across sessions, which still take a new baseline; a liveness bound on a silent JMAP event stream; `DiscoveryServiceConfig::trust`, which the wizard does not show; src/gpeople.rs testing an expired sync token by its 410 alone, where io-gpeople's `is_sync_token_expired` also knows the 400 `EXPIRED_SYNC_TOKEN`.
